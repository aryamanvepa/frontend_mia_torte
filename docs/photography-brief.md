# Photography brief

Every product needs three photographs of the **real product in its real packaging**. Please don't use stock
images, and don't use AI-generated images that change the product, the pack or the label.

| # | Type | Used for | What it shows |
|---|------|----------|---------------|
| 1 | **Pack shot** | Product cards, search, the bag, link previews | The pack front-on, label readable, on a plain warm background. The same light and angle for every product, so the grid looks like one store. |
| 2 | **Close-up** | Product page gallery; a second image on cards when a shopper hovers | The food itself, close: texture, crumb, crunch, ingredients. |
| 3 | **Serving / lifestyle** | Product page gallery, home-page features | The product being eaten or served, in its everyday moment. |

The site crops to a **4:5 portrait** frame, so keep the subject in the centre. Supply files at least 1600 px wide.
The site makes the web sizes itself: `npm run images -- <folder>`.

## Direction by category

- **Cookies and treats:** irresistible. Show a broken cookie with the chocolate pulling, crisp edges, a soft middle, crumbs on the board.
- **Snacks (the lead feature):** crunch and flavour. Show a bowl and a scatter, seasoning visible, a hand reaching in, with tea or a cold drink alongside.
  - The home-page Better Snacking feature needs one wide **lifestyle** shot: Peri Peri Trail Mix and Pudina Millet Crunch on a tea table, hands reaching in.
- **Muesli:** a finished breakfast that looks appetising. A bowl with milk or curd and fresh fruit, plus a dry close-up.
- **Atta and flours:** the result, not the sack. Soft rotis, bhakris or rotlas on a plate, with flour on the board for texture.
- **Dosa mixes and chutney:** a crisp dosa on the tawa or the plate, with chutney or sambar.
